## Axis coverage

- **Display typefaces:** 50 unique, all `bundled` in `src/fonts/_film_font_plan.json`.
- **Background objects:** 50 unique. Full list in the project's `HANDOFF.md` §6.
- **Layout systems:** every pack uses the shared 9:16 column with a per-pack
  `look.<beat>.top` ladder; differentiation is carried by type, world, palette
  and camera rather than by moving the copy column — deliberate, so the reading
  measure is identical across the library and the reading-budget lint is
  comparable pack to pack.
- **Palette strategies:** each palette is a luminance ladder; 22 packs run a light
  ground, 28 a dark or inverted one; every pack exposes exactly 3 hue-rotatable
  slots (`bg`, `accent`, `accent2`).
- **Cycle periods** range 1.6s (Loud Quiet metronome) to 26s (Deep Field
  satellite), so no two worlds beat in sync.
