# Cats Playing Ref — Frame (video / frame layer)

## Overview

Cats Playing Ref is a calm, humane care system: soft off-white grounds, rounded cards, gentle teal/mint washes and one warm accent — for healthcare, wellness, patient and clinical products. Living room cat play — sunbeams on wooden floors, rolling yarn balls, leaping paws, and playful cat toys. The ground is light (#faf4e8); text is
ink #2a1e17 on it. Exactly **one accent
device** (#da0007) per frame marks the single focal point; #ffc845 is a supporting hue. Restraint carries
the identity — a clear type hierarchy, generous space, and one saturated moment beat a busy frame.

## Colors

Ground `#faf4e8`. Text is high-contrast ink. `#da0007` is the one saturated device per frame
(a rule, dot, underline, or the single CTA fill); `#ffc845` supports it. No third saturated hue in a
single frame; never a gradient on body text.

## Typography

- **Display:** Baloo 2, fit-to-measure (≤3 words → heading-xl; 4–6 → heading-lg; 7+ → card-title).
- **Body:** Figtree 400–500; **labels/data:** Figtree caps, 0.2em tracking.
- Legibility floor ≥ 1.3cqw; the display face is the pack's voice — never swap it for a generic sans.

## Frame Treatments

> Recipe: light ground + bokeh texture · one accent device · clear Baloo 2 display moment · generous space.

### 1 · Welcome
A soft-rounded ground; a pill label above a friendly heading, one warm underline, generous air. Nothing sharp.

### 2 · Care
Three rounded cards with a soft icon, a title and a line of body; the middle card carries the warm accent.

### 3 · Outcome
A large rounded figure in the accent with a caption; a soft progress arc behind it.

### 4 · Next step
A centered reassuring sign-off with one soft-filled pill button; plenty of calm space.

## Composition Rules

### Do
- One accent device per frame; everything else in ink/muted on the ground.
- Set display in Baloo 2, fit-to-measure; body in Figtree; labels in Figtree caps.
- Keep frames open and aligned; let the single saturated moment lead the eye.

### Don't
- No second saturated hue in one frame; no gradient on text; no generic-sans display swap.
- Don't crowd the frame or tilt/blur the structure.

## Numerals & Claims

Never invent figures. Stat blocks carry `{metric}` / `±N%` placeholders until the script supplies
values. No real third-party logos or customer marks — render any as a neutral placeholder.

## Known Gaps

- **Baloo 2** + **Figtree** are self-hosted/bundled; the composer keys off the manifest.
- 9:16 / 1:1 are guidance — preserve the whitespace ratio and the display floor.
