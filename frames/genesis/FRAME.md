# Genesis — Frame (the flagship "Living World" film)

**A world, not a slideshow.** Genesis is a dedicated cinematic composer (its own
renderer, like the 3D films) rather than a card system. A persistent, parallax
**living world** sits behind every scene and never stops moving:

- a deep-night sky with a **brand-colored sun** casting animated **god-rays**,
- a three-layer **parallax skyline** of glass towers with **lit windows**,
- **birds** crossing in V-formations and **silhouetted people** walking a ground plane,
- drifting **atmospheric fog**, floating **light motes**, a slow **camera push-in**,
- cinematic **vignette + film grain** finish.

**Eight movie-trailer beats** (routed from the storyboard's scenes):
1. **World intro** — the camera flies into the living city; the headline rises.
2. **Problem** — scattered, tilted, desaturated cards jitter in chaos.
3. **Discovery** — cards fly into an ordered grid; flow lines connect them.
4. **Product reveal** — a floating **screenshot showroom** (a 3D-tilted glass
   browser/device on a glowing pedestal) as converging rays fire behind it.
5. **Features** — floating glass **mini-world** panels, each with an animated icon.
6. **Social proof** — floating avatar chips + **counting metrics** over the crowd.
7. **Growth** — an area+line **graph draws itself into the cityscape**.
8. **CTA** — an epic glowing **logo climax** with a gradient button and a light burst.

**Asset intelligence.** Screenshots become the reveal showroom; extra images fan
into a floating gallery or a drifting content wall; with **no assets** the world +
graphs + type carry the film. Pinned assets (asset.sceneId) claim their scene.

**Brand color.** Nothing is hardcoded — the whole world (sky, sun, towers, windows,
birds, people, glass frames, graphs, particles, buttons, glows) is painted from a
theme derived per-video, so the website's brand color (or a user pick) repaints
everything.

**Type.** Sora (display) + Outfit (subheads) + Inter (body). **Responsive** to
16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5; supports 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 s.

**Best for.** Flagship launches, brand films, product reveals — the most
impressive video a user can make on the platform.
