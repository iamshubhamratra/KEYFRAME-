# Moon Card

A candlelit table under a gold moon — the one pack whose animated world stays lit behind every single beat.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| violet | #1d1430 |
| gold | #d4af6a |
| blush | #e8b4c8 |
| bone | #f2ead9 |

Violet carries Hook, Feature, Stats and App; ink carries Statement, Montage and CTA. Nothing ever inverts to a light ground — the film stays in the dark the whole way through. Montage tiles use a lifted #241a38 with a gold glow.

## Type
Display is Italiana, a very high-contrast display serif, set uppercase at 116px with 0.04em tracking and line-height 1.12 — the widest tracking in the set, which is what keeps a thin serif readable at poster size. Body is Sen.

## World
Five gold moon-phase discs sit in a row at y=180: four are outlined with a terminator arc drawn as a path whose bulge inverts halfway along the row, and the fifth is filled solid — a full moon. Eighteen bone stars are scattered on a fixed lattice and each breathes its own opacity on sin(t + i·1.7). Two tarot cards (112x168, r12, ink fill, 2.5px gold stroke, an inner ring and two gold rules) sit at the lower left and right, each rocking on its own slow sine around a base tilt of plus or minus nine degrees. At the lower right a bone candle column burns with a gold flame ellipse scaled by two summed sines (7Hz and 13.7Hz) over a near-white inner core. Ambient 1.4. World is true on every beat.

## Motion
Cameras zoomOut, drop, pushL, zoomIn, pushU, pushR at multiplier 5, offset 3. Magnitudes: rot 0.4, driftY 9, inn 0.30, driftZ 0.035 — a long soft entry and a slow breathing push-in that never resolves. Titles use drowse (outQuint, 34px lift, no scale); items rise.

## Beats
- **Swipe (flip)** — the card squashes to nothing on its X axis and returns as the next card, with a pill dot pagination underneath; built for three-card draws.
- **Ring (ring)** — a full circular dial with twelve clock ticks and a gold arc sweeping to the value.
- **Morph (fade)** — a 150px word crossfades and scales into the next one, over a thin progress bar.
- **Typing (caret)** — copy types against a thick gold left rule with a blinking pipe, closing on a rotated boxed stamp.
