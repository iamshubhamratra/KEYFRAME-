03 · The scroll's furniture

Seven components, zero boxes

Everything is drawn, not constructed — tapered strokes, irregular ridges, an unclosed circle. The hanko seal is the one sanctioned geometric shape.

始

hanko-seal

The printmaker's stamp on the emphasis word — vermillion rounded square, paper-line inner ring. Exactly one per frame; it lands with a thud.

ink-ridge

Stacked translucent mountain silhouettes on the lower third — far layers lighter and blurred. They drift horizontally, never vertically.

mist-band

Horizontal fog strata that erase the ridge bases. Blurred, slow-drifting, breathing between 50 and 90% opacity.

petal

The only moving pigment — small vermillion petals falling with sinusoidal sway and slow rotation, 6–12 per frame.

brush-rule

A tapered, hand-dragged horizontal stroke — the only divider. Thick at the heel, thin at the lifted tip. Never a straight border.

scroll-margin

A thin vertical gold rule near the left edge — the hanging scroll's cord. Present on every frame; chrome, never content.

ensō circle

One incomplete brush circle for CTA and closing frames. It draws itself on and stops at ~82% — it must never close.

04 · Motion grammar

Unhurried, wet, weighted

Entrances take 0.6–0.9s; the landscape cycles over 8–26s and never syncs with the edit. Nothing snaps — except the seal.

Brushstroke
一

The brush reveal

Each headline word swipes on left→right — a clip-path opening like a loaded brush dragging — while the word settles from a slight skew back to rest. Words stagger like consecutive strokes of one hand. Display type only; subtext simply fades up at 60% ink.

二

The ink-blot cut

Scene transitions are an ink blot: an irregular sumi blob blooms until it swallows the frame, holds a beat, then washes away like ink thinning in water — revealing blank washi an instant before the next words are brushed on. The only moment the frame goes dark.

三

The stamp-thud & the falling world

The hanko arrives from above the paper — scale two-to-one, a one-frame squash, settling at a few degrees of rotation, always after its word finishes revealing. Beneath it all, ridges drift, mist breathes and vermillion petals fall forever, ignoring the edit entirely.
