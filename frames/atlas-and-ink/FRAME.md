# Atlas & Ink

A cartography studio at work: the route on the backdrop literally draws itself in step with the scene.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| parch | #efe4cf |
| sepia | #4a3826 |
| compass | #a8402e |
| sea | #6a92a8 |
| gold | #c9a24b |

Parchment is the ground for Hook, Feature, Stats and App. Statement and Montage invert to sepia with parchment type and a gold highlight. The CTA is the one saturated frame — solid compass rust with a parchment block button. Radii are the tightest in the family: paper cards at r6, montage tiles at r4.

## Type
Display is Young Serif — a chunky old-style serif with one weight — set in sentence case at 118px, line-height 1.08. Body is Assistant. The backdrop reuses Young Serif italic for its one hand-lettered marginal note.

## World
Five horizontal and four vertical sepia rules at 10% opacity grid the entire frame like a chart graticule. A sea-tinted landmass blob (16% sea fill, 35% sepia stroke) sits at mid-left. Over it a rust dashed voyage route (18/14 dash) is revealed by dash-offset keyed to scene progress between 6% and 85% — it draws itself, starting from a solid rust dot, and its arrowhead only becomes visible once the draw passes 95%. At the lower right a compass rose rocks eight degrees on a 0.5Hz sine: two concentric sepia rings, four cardinal darts with the N and S arms in rust, and a gold centre pin. At the lower left a sepia sea-serpent squiggle with an eye dot bobs six pixels, beside the italic line "hic sunt dracones". Ambient 1.5.

## Motion
Cameras zoomOut, pushL, drop, pushR, zoomIn, hopU at multiplier 5, offset 0. Magnitudes: rot 0.4, driftX 5, inn 0.28 — the smallest drift and the longest settle in the set. Titles drowse; items rise.

## Beats
- **Typing (typewriter)** — each character sets at its own rotation of up to 1.3 degrees and a 2px vertical jitter, under a blinking underscore, closing on a double-ruled rotated stamp.
- **DragDrop (assemble)** — a chip flies in on an arc, lands in its slot, shakes on a damped sine and reports "locked in".
- **Ring (gauge)** — a half-circle needle dial for plate progress and completion.
- **Scroll (board)** — numbered rows with dotted leaders and flickering values; commission and studio ledgers.

## Best for
Map and print studios, publishers and independent bookshops, museums, archives and libraries, heritage travel and expedition brands, whisky, leather and other slow goods, field research and survey work.
