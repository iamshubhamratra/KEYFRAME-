## Composition rules

- IT'S A PAGE: ruled lines + red margin always visible; content sits ON the paper.
  Type is warm serif journal writing (typewriter entrance), never techno.
- EVERYTHING DRAWS ITSELF: doodles arrive stroke-by-stroke (dashoffset draw-on),
  then keep living — stars twinkle, rays blink, steam rises, bunting sways,
  the plane bobs. No static clipart, no dead frames.
- INK FIRST, COLOR AS PEN ACCENTS: ink outlines everywhere; red/blue/green pen +
  one yellow highlighter carry the color. Fills are rare and translucent.
- HAND WOBBLE: boxes are crooked, lines are squiggles, circles are imperfect —
  perfection is off-brand.
- NO dashboards, kicker chips, giant counting numbers, gradients or glows.

## Scene treatments

- HOOK: heading-xl left; a LIGHTBULB with blinking rays top-right; a PAPER PLANE
  swoops on a dashed path; washi tape, stars, ink splat, margin ticks.
- PROOF/STAT: the headline states the number ("38% faster" highlighted); below, a
  HAND-DRAWN GRAPH draws itself — squiggle axis, X-mark nodes popping, a red
  wobbly line joining them, an arrowhead landing; a bullseye pulses top-right.
- FEATURE/HOW: crooked CHECKLIST boxes draw in, red checkmarks scribble on,
  squiggle handwriting lines; a coffee cup steams in the corner.
- CLOSE/CTA: party BUNTING draws + sways across the top; the message gets a pen
  scribble CIRCLED TWICE around it; curly doodle arrows point in from the corners.

## Don't

- No gradients, glows, neon, 3D, stock dashboards or perfect geometry.
- Emphasis is highlighter + wavy underline — never boxed, gradient or glowing.
- Don't overfill: it's a journal page, not a poster — the paper must breathe.
