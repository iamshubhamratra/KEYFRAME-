# Ember Roast

Ember Roast — a small-batch roastery in cocoa and cream, lit by one burnt ember.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| roast | #2b1d16 | primary ground on hook, feature, stats, app |
| cream | #f3e9dc | body and headline type on dark grounds |
| ember | #e0662c | the single highlight; floods the CTA ground |
| gold | #caa465 | second accent — statement highlight, tile labels, beans |
| ink | #1c110c | statement/montage ground, card fill, type on ember |

## Type
Display: **Alfa Slab One** — one weight, heavy slab, set uppercase at 96–154px. Body: **Karla** 400–800 for kickers, chips, stat labels and subs. Titles run uppercase on every beat.

## World
An SVG layer that runs on hook, feature, stats and app beats (off on statement, montage and CTA). Three cream steam curls — S-shaped `q` paths, 14px round-cap stroke — climb 700px from the base on a 0.24Hz loop, fading to zero as they rise. Lower right: a 120r gold ring holding eight ember spokes that rotate at 1.4 rad/s around a solid 30r ember hub — the roaster's cooling tray. Seven gold bean ellipses (12×17) fall the full height on a 0.3Hz loop, each rotating 260° plus a per-index offset. Ambient clock 1.7.

## Motion
Camera set: pushL → zoomIn → hopU → pushR → zoomOut → drop, at multiplier 5 with offset 1. Magnitudes: 0.9° roll, 7px x-drift, 8px y-drift. Titles enter on **slam**, items on **pop**. Motion default: Lively.

## Beats
- **Ring — gauge**: a needle sweep to a target percentage; used for roast curve / to-first-crack.
- **Scroll — board**: a chalkboard list that scrolls a menu of items and prices.
- **Toggle — switch**: a two-state flip for dialling in a setting.
- **Morph — fade**: soft cross-dissolve between named options (bags, origins).
- Plus the shared spine: Hook (ember pill kicker), Statement (ink ground, gold highlight), Feature (tilted ink card, ember/gold pills), Montage (four tiles tilted −2.5° to 2°), Stats, CTA (full ember ground, ink pill button, circular logo).

## Best for
Coffee roasters, bakeries, distillers, chocolate and spice makers, neighbourhood hospitality — brands whose product is heat, smell and a daily batch.
