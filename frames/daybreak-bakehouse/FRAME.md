# Daybreak Bakehouse

**The morning bake, filmed.** A warm cream stage lit by a low golden sun, a
counter slab with a forever-steaming cup, dust motes drifting in one raking
window beam, and terracotta pendants swaying overhead. Round Caprasimo
headlines drift up like steam; one word per headline gets a warm marker swipe.

## The rules

1. **Warm cream, always.** The ground is paper `#f5ead8`; surfaces are
   `#ebddc5`. Terracotta `#c67139` is the voice, sage `#7a8a5e` the herb note,
   dawn `#f7d9b8` the light.
2. **One marker word.** Exactly one word per headline carries the terracotta
   marker swipe. The rest stays ink.
3. **The counter grounds everything.** Every scene stands on the counter band;
   the steam never stops.
4. **Drift like steam.** Headlines rise softly out of a blur; cuts iris like
   morning light opening. Nothing snaps, nothing bounces hard.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** The day's board, the batch counts, the
   opening hour — all lifted from the brief, never invented.

## Purpose

Bakeries, cafés, farmers markets, craft and food brands, morning rituals —
anything that should feel baked before the town wakes up.
