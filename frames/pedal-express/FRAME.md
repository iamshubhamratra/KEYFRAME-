# Pedal Express

A courier film: the city scrolls past, the wheel never stops, and every line of type arrives leaning forward.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| asphalt | #26282c |
| orange | #ff6b35 |
| cyan | #4cc9f0 |
| chalk | #f2f4f6 |
| ink | #1b1d21 |

Asphalt is the working ground. Statement takes the whole frame in orange with ink type at 152px. Montage drops to ink with asphalt tiles and cyan labels. CTA inverts to full cyan with a hard ink block button.

## Type
Display is Passion One — condensed heavy poster caps, 128px on Hook, tracked slightly open at 0.01em, always uppercase. Body is Fira Sans across four weights, doing chips, ledger rows and notification copy.

## World
Five dark ink slabs of varying width and height scroll right-to-left at 620px/s at three depths (0.55 down to 0.31 alpha) — a skyline in parallax. At the lower left a chalk bicycle wheel: a 9px-stroke rim of radius 90, six spokes rotating at 5 rad/s, and a solid orange hub. Three cyan speed streaks of decreasing weight dart across at 900px/s on a 500px loop. An orange dashed route (22/16 dash, offset crawling at -90t) curves down the frame from a solid cyan waypoint dot, and a small orange arrow marker with an ink dot sits mid-frame like a drop pin. Ambient 2.0. World runs on Hook, Feature, Stats and App.

## Motion
Cameras pushL, pushR, zoomIn, pushU, spin, drop at multiplier 7, offset 0 — a different move nearly every beat. Magnitudes: skew 5, rot 1, slide 0.34, inn 0.17, so entries are quick and the frame shears as it settles. Both title and item presets are streak: 150px translateX plus a -12deg skewX under outExpo.

## Beats
- **Ring (gauge)** — a half-circle dial with a swinging needle, five tick marks and a counted number; ETA and speed readouts.
- **Scroll (ticker)** — three rows of display type marquee in alternating directions at different sizes and speeds, footed by an entry count and a live flag.
- **Notify (side)** — cards slide in from the right edge, each with a bar at its base that drains as its life runs out.
- **DragDrop (drag)** — a pointer grabs a chip, arcs it into a dashed slot, and a check badge pops beside it.

## Best for
Bike couriers and last-mile delivery, logistics and fleet software, urban mobility and e-bike brands, on-demand services, city sport and streetwear, anything that sells speed as the product.
