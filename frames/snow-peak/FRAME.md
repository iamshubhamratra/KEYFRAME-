# Snow Peak

An alpine morning in sentence case — a ridgeline, a gondola that really rides its cable, and the calmest camera in the family.

## Type
Fjalla One at 98–158px — and uniquely in this family, **no beat sets `upper`**, so titles stay in sentence case and read as signage and lift-station copy rather than a headline shout. Hind (400–700) carries body, checklist rows and stat labels. Statement beats go white on pine; montage goes white on the near-black `deep`.

## World
A single continuous mountain scene, shown on hook, feature and CTA. A five-vertex pine ridgeline fills the lower half, its two tallest summits at 30% and 74% width each given a separately drawn white cap with a concave underside. A darker `deep` drift curve sweeps in front as the near slope. A single ink line runs from (60, 560) up to the right edge at 380 — the cable. A coral gondola cabin with a hanger arm and an ice-blue window travels that cable right-to-left at 60 units per second, its y tracking the 0.19 cable gradient exactly and swaying 6px on sin(t·1.4). Eighteen snowflakes fall at four speeds with a sin(t·0.8) sideways wander and three sizes. Ambient clock 1.6.

## Motion
Six-move set — drop, pushR, zoomOut, pushL, hopU, zoomIn — strided by 5 from offset 4, walking the list in reverse across all six. Magnitudes are the gentlest here: roll 0.6, vertical drift 10, nothing else. Titles `rise`, items `pop`. The pale ice ground returns on hook, feature and CTA so the film keeps opening back out to sky.

## Beats
- **Toggle (check)** — gear checks, cabin shutdown lists, après confirmations as ticked rows.
- **Ring (ring)** — a clean progress arc for climbs, season-pass math, day completion.
- **DragDrop (drag)** — items lifted into a pack; "pack the essentials", "wax the skis".
- **Morph (roll)** — one term rolling into the next, for snow conditions and run names.
- Feature cards are `frame` (deep fill, snow rule, radius 22) with coral/pine pill chips; montage tiles are #16272f with snow rules and ±2° tilts.

## Best for
Ski resorts and lift companies, alpine and outdoor gear, mountain cabins and lodges, booking and pass products, winter sports teams, and any brand selling an early, quiet, well-prepared start.
