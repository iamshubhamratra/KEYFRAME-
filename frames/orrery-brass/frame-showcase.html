Plate IV

The Instruments

Seven components, all hairline — 1–2px strokes always, never thick bars. Each demo runs on its own escapement.

discovery

orbit-ring

A slightly rotated ellipse circles the emphasis word — an astronomer marking a discovery. One per frame.

brass-hand

The pointer advances in discrete escapement ticks — steps(12), never a smooth sweep.

escapement-tick

Graduation scales on arcs, rules, and frame edges — the instrument's decoration.

cartouche

moon-phase-dots

engraved-flourish

Fine corner filigree — two opposing corners of a frame, never all four.

Plate V

Motion Grammar

Everything moves like a mechanism under tension, released in measured beats. Easing vocabulary: elastic (pendulums), steps(n) (hands & wipes), linear (orbits). No bounce, no neon flash.

pendulum

IPendulum entrance

Headline words swing in from a pivot high above the word (origin 50% −150%), settling with an elastic ease — a released escapement coming to rest.

IIClock wipe

Scene transitions sweep the frame conically behind a rotating brass hand — in discrete ticks, steps(24), tooth by tooth. Never a plain crossfade.

IIIOrrery ambient

Planets run their elliptical rings at non-harmonic periods; the sun-pivot breathes. The instrument runs — it never dances.

IVOrbit-ring emphasis

After its word settles, the ellipse draws on clockwise like a hand-circled note on a chart, then precesses imperceptibly.
