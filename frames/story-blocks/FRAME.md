# Story Blocks

**Every brand has a story. Tell it in blocks.** Bold panels slide in from
alternating sides, kinetic left-set Caprasimo springs in word by word, and
whip-pans cut chapter to chapter.

## The rules

1. **One idea per panel.** A block carries a single beat — never two.
2. **Alternate the sides.** Panels enter left, then right, then left.
3. **One bracketed word.** Exactly one terracotta-bracket word per headline.
4. **Keep it alive.** Landed words micro-wobble; the conveyor keeps moving;
   whip-pans throw the cuts.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** Numbers with weight, lifted from the
   brief.

## Purpose

Brand stories, explainers, product narratives — bold, fast, impossible to
skim past.
