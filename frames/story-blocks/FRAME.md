# Story Blocks

An **edited-video** look: nothing is ever parked. Colour panels travel in from alternating sides, tilted ticker rails march opposite ways, starbursts turn — and every scene lands on a different full-field colour block.

## Palette
- **Ground** `#F5EAD8`, **ink / paper** `#201E1D`.
- **Accents** `#C67139` · `#7A8A5E` · `#D9B44A`.
- Under a brand skin the ENTIRE world recolours — every element above, at the pack's own
  authored luminances, so the film keeps its value structure and takes the brand's hue.

## Type
- **Display** Caprasimo. **Body** Figtree — the source pack’s own faces.

## Motion
Six beats — hook, statement, feature, montage, stats, close — each with its own camera
move running the full scene, entrances that rise and pop, and a **wipe** cut.

## Screenshots
Aspect-routed from the asset's real pixels: a browser frame for a desktop capture (which
**scrolls** its page inside the window), a phone for a mobile one, an outlined card
otherwise. Two or more captures become the tile wall.

## Best for
Editorial, media, sport, culture and campaign brands.
