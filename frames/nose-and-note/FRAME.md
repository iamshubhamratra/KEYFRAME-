# Nose & Note

A bespoke-perfume atelier: ivory paper, deep plum floods, bronze hairlines, and a camera that refuses to hurry.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| ivory (paper ground) | `#f6f0e8` |
| plum (accent / flood) | `#4a2438` |
| bronze (kicker, rings, droplets) | `#a86e3c` |
| blush (highlight on dark grounds) | `#e8c4b8` |
| ink (deepest ground, CTA) | `#2e1a26` |
| desk (stage surround) | `#2e1a26` |

Hook / Feature / Stats / App sit on ivory with ink type and plum highlights. Statement inverts to a full plum ground with ivory type and blush highlights at 140px. Montage is plum with ivory tiles. CTA floods ink with a blush pill button and a circular logo mark.

## Type

**Gilda Display** (serif) for every title, line-height 1.08 — high-contrast, unhurried, no letterspacing tricks. **Albert Sans** 400–700 for kickers, captions, chips and body. Nothing is uppercased; the pack relies on the serif's own authority rather than caps.

## World

Runs on Hook, Feature, Stats and App; the plum Statement and ink CTA beats go clean.

- Three capped bottles at the right edge, sizes 64×90 / 46×64 / 40×56, filled plum, bronze and blush at 80% with a bronze cap block and a white 50% highlight dot each.
- Three scent trails — doubled S-curves — rise 500px on a 0.16 clock, plum at 25% alpha fading to zero as they climb, phase-offset by a third each.
- A fan of five blotter strips at x=190, angled -24° to +24°, the centre strip blush and the rest ink at 12% with a 30% outline; the whole fan rocks ±3° at 0.8Hz.
- Three bronze droplets bob on a 1.2Hz sine with decreasing radii.
- A 60px bronze ring strokes itself in between 10% and 80% of the beat via dash-offset — the maceration timer.

## Motion

Cams `zoomOut, pushL, drop, pushU, zoomIn, pushR` at multiplier 3, offset 1. Magnitudes are the smallest in the set: 0.3° roll, 4px X drift, 0.03 Z. Titles use **drowse** (outQuint over 46% of the beat, 34px lift); items **rise**. Default energy **Calm** (0.6). Ambient world clock 1.4.

## Beats

- **Swipe → flip** — a single panel flips on its vertical axis to the next face, with a dot pager beneath. Used for scent options.
- **Ring → ring** — full 240px circular dial with twelve tick marks and the number centred. Reads as a maceration clock.
- **Typing → caret** — text types against a 10px accent rule with a blinking bar, closing on a rotated stamp badge. The formula.
- **Notify → side** — toasts slide in from the right, each with a draining accent bar. Atelier notes.
- Plus Hook, Statement (plum flood), Feature (framed plum card, outline chips), Montage (near-flat ±1.5° tilts), Stats, Morph and CTA.

## Best for

Fragrance houses, skincare and beauty, fine jewellery, small-batch spirits, spas and retreats, editorial beauty features — any brief where restraint is the selling point.
