# Ghost Route

A night ghost-walk: fog that never clears, one amber lantern, and a phosphor-mint glow on everything the tour touches.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| fog (primary ground) | `#1a2026` |
| spectral (glow accent) | `#9fe8c4` |
| lantern (warm accent) | `#e8a23c` |
| bone (type) | `#ece8dc` |
| ink (deepest ground) | `#12161a` |
| desk (stage surround) | `#10141a` |

Hook / Feature / Stats / App on fog; Statement, Montage and CTA drop to ink. Every ground is dark — there is no paper beat in this pack. Montage tiles use `#20262e` with a spectral glow and spectral labels; the app card ground is `#232b33`.

## Type

**Grenze Gotisch** 500–700 for titles, line-height 1.06 — condensed gothic display, mixed case. **Spectral** 400–600 serif for kickers, captions and body: the only serif-on-serif pairing in this group, and it reads as a printed guidebook rather than an app.

## World

**Runs on every beat** — Hook, Statement, Feature, Montage, Stats, CTA and App. Unique to this pack: the atmosphere is never switched off.

- Two ghosts (bell body with a three-scallop hem and two ink eyes, spectral at 50%) rise 500px on a 0.12 clock, swaying ±40px, opacity falling from 0.58 to 0.08 as they climb.
- A hanging lantern at the right: a 4px bone cord, a 44×56 open frame, and an amber flame circle whose opacity pulses 0.45–0.95 at 6Hz, the whole rig rocking ±10° at 1.3Hz.
- Three headstones at x=140, 260 and W-320 — rounded ink slabs at 95% with a 20%-bone outline and a hairline cross-mark.
- Two bone-tinted mist bands: a 130px sheet at 7% breathing up and down on a 0.4 clock, and a fixed 80px sheet at 10% along the ground.
- Eight stars at fixed positions twinkling between 0.3 and 0.7 alpha.

## Motion

Cams `zoomOut, drop, pushL, zoomIn, pushU, pushR` at multiplier 5, offset 3. Magnitudes: 0.5° roll, 10px Y drift, 0.28 inner, 0.04 Z — a slow vertical float with a dolly, not a snap. Titles use **drowse** (outQuint over 46% of the beat); items **rise**. Default energy **Calm** (0.6). Ambient world clock 1.5.

## Beats

- **Scroll → feed** — a tall panel with numbered rows scrolling under a live scrollbar thumb. The sightings log.
- **Ring → gauge** — a 180° speedometer arc with tick marks and a needle that swings from -90°, number below. The EMF meter.
- **Swipe → swipe** — a three-card stack; the top card flies right and rotates 18° with a stamp rotated 12° in the corner while the next promotes forward. Choose your route.
- **Typing → caret** — text types against a 10px accent rule with a blinking bar, closed by a rotated stamp. The guide's note.
- Plus Hook (outline amber kicker), Statement, Feature (glow card on ink), Montage (glowing tiles), Stats with **glowNums**, and a glowing spectral CTA button.

## Best for

Ghost and history walking tours, haunted attractions, horror podcasts and fiction, escape rooms, Halloween events, folklore and true-crime content, late-night bars with a story.
