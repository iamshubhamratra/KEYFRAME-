# Hype Wave

**Make some actual noise.** Cream paper, navy ink, electric blue / taxi yellow
/ coral stickers, and Archivo Black mega-type that slams in word by word with
scrolling checkerboards and spinning stars around it.

## The rules

1. **Cream ground, navy ink, three accents.** Blue is the voice, yellow the
   flash, coral the spark. Never a fourth accent.
2. **Slam, don't fade.** Words drop with squash overshoot; stickers snap and
   wobble; cuts flash. Beige easing is a choice — refuse it.
3. **Every third word outlined.** The display alternates filled/accent/outlined.
4. **Stickers have hard shadows.** Solid navy offset, no blur, slight rotation.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** Share counts and watch stats are lifted
   from the brief — never invented.

## Purpose

Drops, launches, events, promos, creators — ten loud seconds, zero boring frames.
