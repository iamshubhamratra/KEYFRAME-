# Hype Wave

**Electric sticker pop.** Checkerboard rails scroll, wavy ribbons cross the frame, star stickers spin with thick outlines, and the field swaps colour every scene. Archivo Black slams in uppercase over the lot.

## Palette
- **Ground** `#2B5BFF`, **ink / paper** `#101433`.
- **Accents** `#FFD234` · `#FF5A48` · `#2B5BFF`.
- Under a brand skin the ENTIRE world recolours — every element above, at the pack's own
  authored luminances, so the film keeps its value structure and takes the brand's hue.

## Type
- **Display** Archivo Black. **Body** Space Grotesk.

## Motion
Six beats — hook, statement, feature, montage, stats, close — each with its own camera
move running the full scene, entrances that rise and pop, and a **blinds** cut.

## Screenshots
Aspect-routed from the asset's real pixels: a browser frame for a desktop capture (which
**scrolls** its page inside the window), a phone for a mobile one, an outlined card
otherwise. Two or more captures become the tile wall.

## Best for
Youth, creator, sport, gaming, music and DTC brands.
