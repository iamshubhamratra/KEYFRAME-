# SOLE DROP

Sole Drop — a release-day scoreboard in court black, acid volt and flame.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| court | #101014 | primary ground on hook, feature, stats, app |
| volt | #d4f24b | the highlight; floods the statement ground entirely |
| chalk | #f4f4f2 | type on dark grounds, montage tile lines, the outline counter |
| flame | #ff5a36 | second accent — statement highlight, chips, the bouncing ball |
| ink | #0a0a0c | montage and CTA ground, card fill, type on volt |

## Type
Display: **Archivo Black** at 96–158px, uppercase everywhere, letter-spaced 0.01em. Body: **Archivo** 400–700. The world layer reuses Archivo Black for its outline counter, so the type is part of the backdrop.

## World
Runs on hook, feature, stats, CTA and app. Four volt speed lines sweep left-to-right at 500px/s, stacked 90px apart, each thinner and fainter than the last. A flame zigzag path — twelve 60px segments alternating ±44px — runs across the lower frame like tread. A 30r flame ball travels the full width on a 0.9Hz loop, arcing up to 260px on abs(sin) with an ink stitched cross-seam drawn on it. Top right: a two-digit counter in Archivo Black 150px, stroke-only chalk at 14% opacity, incrementing twice a second. Ambient clock 2.0 — the busiest backdrop in this group.

## Motion
Camera set: pushR → zoomIn → pushU → spin → pushL → drop, multiplier 7 (top tier), offset 2. Magnitudes: 5° skew, 1.1° roll, 0.17 inner scale, 0.3 slide. Titles enter on **streak**, items on **slam**. Motion default: Bouncy.

## Beats
- **Cursor — keys**: a keyboard-driven cursor runs a checkout speedrun across a form.
- **Notify — pop**: notification cards punch in and out (restock, size alerts).
- **Swipe — swipe**: a horizontal carousel for colourways.
- **Ring — bar**: a linear claim/stock bar filling to a target, not a dial.
- Plus the spine: Hook (volt tag kicker), Statement (full volt ground, ink type, flame highlight), Feature (10r framed ink card, square volt/flame/chalk chips), Montage (four tiles, zero tilt, 8px radius), Stats (with rule), CTA (ink ground, volt block button, rounded logo).

## Best for
Sneaker and streetwear drops, limited releases, ticketed launches, esports and sport retail — anything sold against a countdown.
