# Lantern Night

**The night, filmed.** An ink sky over shimmering water, glowing paper
lanterns rising past a haloed moon, fireflies in the margins — and Caprasimo
headlines in warm cream that swing in like hung lanterns, one word glowing.

## The rules

1. **Dark, but warm.** The ground is deep night `#171310`; type is paper
   `#f5ead8`; the light is lantern `#c67139` and glow `#e8b06a`; fireflies are
   sage `#7a8a5e`. Never cold blue, never pure black.
2. **One glowing word.** Exactly one word per headline glows amber with a soft
   halo. The rest stays paper-cream.
3. **The night never freezes.** A lantern is always rising; stars twinkle;
   reflections shimmer. Ambient motion is the world's heartbeat.
4. **Swing, settle, glow.** Words pendulum in and settle; cuts bloom warm.
   Bouncy but hushed — festival, not carnival.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** Lantern counts, showtimes and proof
   points are lifted from the brief — never invented.

## Purpose

Festivals, events, night launches, hospitality, celebrations — for stories
that come alive when the sun goes down.
