# Digital Universe

A **prompt-to-video launch film** set INSIDE a **data-cosmos** — you are inside a
computer, in a living data-space, not looking at a literal starry sky. A persistent,
continuously-flowing backdrop runs behind every scene and **matches across every cut**,
so the film reads as one unbroken flight through the machine rather than a slideshow.
Portrait-native **9:16** for Reels, Shorts and TikTok (it also holds up in 1:1 and 16:9 —
all geometry is relative to the stage).

## The data-space (persistent, theme-colored)
- **Ground** near-black `#05070F`. Nothing is a flat fill: the whole backdrop is painted
  on one canvas as a pure function of time (deterministic, seek-exact).
- **Particle depth field** — a volume of data-point particles flies slowly toward the
  camera through a perspective tunnel, with depth, drift and twinkle (accent-tinted).
- **Network connecting-lines** — faint accent lines are strung between the nearest bright
  particles each frame, so the field reads as a live data graph, not loose dust.
- **3D data-grid corridor** — a dotted lattice recedes on a floor and a mirrored ceiling,
  converging to a central vanishing point and scrolling forward.
- **Data-nebula glows** — two soft accent clouds drift behind everything.
- **Vignette** — darkens the edges back into the ground so content always owns the center.

This is deliberately **distinct from Kinetic Universe** (which is big soft light beams + a
single floor grid + a parallax starfield): Digital Universe is a **particle/network/grid
data-volume** — more "inside a computer" than "out in space".

## Palette — everything is theme-driven
Default accents: **electric blue** `#4F7CFF` → **data teal** `#22E0C8` → **violet**
`#A66BFF`. `primary` and `secondary` derive the **gradient** (`120deg primary→secondary`),
the panel **surface** (`color-mix primary 12% over #0B0E1A`), the **border**, the **grid /
network line** color and the **glow**. Because the whole system is derived from the
accents, a brand color repaints the ENTIRE universe — particles, grid, network lines, data
panels, chips, buttons and gradient headlines — not just the text. Text is `#EEF1FF` with a
66%/36% dim scale for supporting copy.

## Type
- **Display** Space Grotesk 800, tight (`-0.03em`), often clipped to the accent
  **gradient** (kinetic gradient headlines that pop in word-by-word).
- **Chrome / data** JetBrains Mono (kickers, chips, the CTA URL).
- **Body** Inter, dimmed.

## Motion grammar (three curves)
- **Enter** ease-out — slides and scales into place.
- **Pop** back-out — punchy overshoot reveals (headline words, buttons, the logo mark).
- **Drift** sine — ambient float on panels, chips, the data-core and the mark.
Every scene fades from and back to pure data-space at its edges (opacity 0 at the
boundary), so cuts frame-match. Signature beats: a **data-core** forms with a flash on the
opener; data **streams converge** into a shockwave before the headline; screenshot
**data-panels float**; the logo **assembles** and the wordmark **types** in on the CTA.

## Screenshots & images (intentional slots)
Real screenshots ride in glowing **data-panel** device-frames (`.du-panel`): rounded,
surface-filled, brand-bordered, with **HUD corner brackets**, a top sheen and a scan-line.
- **Showcase** — a hero panel (or a small 2–3 panel perspective wall) with the headline
  and feature chips. Portrait screenshots (ratio < 0.9) get a tall phone frame; wider
  shots get a landscape/dashboard card.
- **Scatter** — several panels **scatter and float** around a centered caption.
- With **no** screenshot, a panel shows an intentional branded placeholder (gradient wash
  + holographic grid + a glowing node), never a blank box.
- An uploaded **logo** is reserved for the CTA mark (never a data panel).

## Scene types (chosen per storyboard scene)
`open` (opener: forming data-core + title), `reveal` (converging data streams + kinetic
gradient headline, for stats/impact), `showcase` (data-panels + features), `scatter`
(scattered floating panels), `orbit` (headline + rising feature chips, the bullet default),
`statement` (a single breathing gradient line, for quotes), `cta` (logo reveal + typed
wordmark + gradient button). Any scene count and duration works; the data-space is constant.

## Do
- Let the data-space flow — keep scene content centered inside the safe area (Reels chrome).
- Use the gradient for the hero headline and the CTA; keep body copy in the dim scale.
- Give real screenshots the glowing data-panel; keep the logo for the CTA mark.

## Don't
- Don't add a second full-frame background — the one canvas data-space is the world.
- Don't hard-code a hex; take every color from the derived theme so brand color carries.
- Don't overfill a portrait frame — a few words per headline, ≤5 chips, ≤3 hero panels.
