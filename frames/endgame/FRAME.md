# Endgame

Chess-club pack: parchment and walnut, one brick-red accent, and a board that plays itself in the corner.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| board | `#efe6d8` | primary ground (hook, feature, stats, app), tiles, CTA button |
| ink | `#221c16` | copy, statement/montage ground |
| walnut | `#6b4a2f` | feature card, kicker, CTA ground, board squares |
| check | `#b3452e` | highlight, knight, fast clock hand, lead stat |
| sage | `#7d8c69` | third stat column, cool counterweight |

## Type

Display: **DM Serif Display** — line-height 1.08, titles 92–148px. Body: **DM Sans** 400–700. Small radii throughout (card 10, tile 8) keep the whole thing feeling printed rather than app-like.

## World

Shown on hook, feature, stats and app. A 5×5 checkerboard of 84px squares fills the bottom-right in 30%-opacity walnut, framed by a heavy 6px walnut border. A knight piece in check red with an ink outline hops a four-square circuit on a 2s loop, each move eased in-out with a 60px sine arc so it genuinely leaps. Top-left, an analogue chess clock: a rounded rect split by a centre line, its left hand sweeping at 2.2 rad/s in check red, its right creeping at 0.4 rad/s in ink. Along the base, ♟ ♞ ♛ set in DM Serif Display brighten one at a time on a 0.7Hz rotation. Ambient clock 1.5.

## Motion

Cameras: zoomIn → pushL → drop → pushR → zoomOut → hopU, multiplier 5, offset 2. Magnitude rot 0.5, inner 0.26, driftX 5 — restrained lateral movement. Titles enter on **drowse**, items on **flip**. Default motion preset is Lively, so cuts land with intent even though the frame stays calm. Montage tilts are all `0` — deliberately square.

## Beats

- **Scroll / board** — a standings ladder scrolls as a ranked board.
- **Toggle / check** — a prep checklist ticks item by item.
- **Typing / typewriter** — text strikes on character by character; the deck uses it for move annotation.
- **Morph / flap** — a split-flap word change, the pack's signature reveal.

## Best for

Chess and board-game clubs, leagues and tournaments, adult-education courses, bookshops and libraries, strategy and analytics brands, and any membership with a weekly ritual.
