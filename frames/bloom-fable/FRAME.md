# Bloom Fable

A **pastel storybook** design system where the product grows like a plant. The film
opens on a soft sky-to-cream **meadow** — a smiling sun with turning rays, drifting
clouds, rolling hills, swaying grass and floating sparkles — and each scene is a
hand-drawn chapter: a flower that **blooms** petal by petal, a fluttering butterfly,
draw-on vines, a rainbow, ribbons and falling petals. Nothing mechanical — organic
draw-ons, elastic blooms and gentle sine sways.

## Palette
- **Meadow** sky `#E4F1F2` → cream `#FBF4E8` gradient ground.
- **Ink** warm plum `#46345A`, body `#6C5B80`.
- **Coral** `#E8705F` — the primary accent (emphasis words, ribbons, CTA).
- **Sun** `#F2B95C`, **sage** `#7FA876`, **sky** `#A9D7E8` — the supporting garden.

## Type
- **Display** Fraunces (warm serif, gentle) for headlines, chapter marks, the brand.
- **Body/chrome** Inter. Chapter kickers are Fraunces *italic* ("script").

## Motion
- Word-by-word rise, draw-on flourishes/vines (`pathLength`), elastic petal blooms,
  count-up stat rings, a butterfly path, petal rain, a growing rainbow. 400–900ms,
  soft eases (back / elastic / power3). Deterministic (paused seeked timeline).

## Screenshots
Real screenshots/photos are presented as **framed cream cards** with a soft shadow
and a leaf-tag label — a picture pinned into the storybook — with a gentle grow-in
and Ken-Burns.

## Best for
Warm, human, approachable brands — wellness, education, family, lifestyle, community,
sustainable and consumer products.
