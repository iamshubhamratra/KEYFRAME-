03 · Components

Seven living organisms

Every component is alive by contract — ambient loops run unsynced, because synchronized water reads as machinery.

caustic-web · the signature

Overlapping cyan sine filaments, each undulating on its own 7–14s period. Lives in the top third — surface light, 4000 m overhead.

bubble-column

Stroked foam circles rise the full frame with a gentle sine sway, popping near the top. One or two loose columns, off the type's measure.

plankton-drift

The living dust of the water column — motes drifting on slow vectors, each pulsing on a 2–5s heartbeat. Rarely, one is magenta.

jellyfish

A soft magenta dome with a cyan rim light and trailing tentacles. The bell pulses every ~2.6s; each pulse propels a slow drift. One per frame, at most.

sonar-ring

The attention ping — concentric strokes expanding 3–5× and fading over 2–3s, repeating while the target holds the frame. One source per frame.

04 · Motion grammar

Motion is the water

Everything at this depth is slow. Nothing linear, nothing snappier than 0.4s except the elastic overshoot; exits sink — down, fade, blur — never pop.

1 · Ripple-in

Headline words arrive refracted: vertically stretched (scaleY ≈ 2.4), skewed, blurred and dim, then wobble elastically into focus — scale, skew and blur resolving together on elastic.out(1, 0.45), staggered so the line un-refracts left to right. After arrival, type keeps a barely-visible drift; nothing at depth is perfectly still.

2 · Submerge

Scenes do not cut — they sink. A tide with a curved, cyan-lit wave crest rises from the bottom and rolls up over the frame, carrying the old scene down beneath it; as the crest clears the top, the next scene is already in the water, its headline beginning to ripple in. Never a straight wipe.

3 · Living water

The ambient layer never stops: caustic filaments undulate on unsynced periods, plankton pulses on individual heartbeats, bubbles rise and pop, and a jellyfish may propel itself through the middle distance. Synchronized loops are a violation — the sea has no metronome.

4 · Sonar echo

The emphasis word lands with the ripple grammar, then its magenta and cyan ghosts split outward to their offsets and keep swimming on a slow sine, while sonar rings ping from the word every 4–6s for as long as it holds the frame.
