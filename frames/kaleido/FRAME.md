# Kaleido — Frame (video design system)

**World.** A kaleidoscope barrel. Everything is radially symmetric and slowly
turning around the frame's center: a six-fold mirrored field of drifting prism
shapes (the animated background), concentric rings breathing outward from the
core, a faint rotating spoke halo, orbiting satellite dots, and — on the opening
and closing beats — a central mandala that unfurls petal ring by petal ring.

**Motion grammar (all bespoke to this pack).**
- **Entrance `bloom`** — each headline word unfurls from a near-zero scale with a
  rotational settle around its base, like a petal opening.
- **Emphasis `prism`** — the accent word is clipped by a three-hue gradient
  (accent → accent2 → extra) and circled by a hand-drawn orbit ring.
- **Cut `shatter`** — a disc of accent-tinted wedges scales up spinning to cover
  the frame, then spins back out: the kaleidoscope turning between shots.
- **Canvas `kaleido`** — a true six-fold mirrored kaleidoscope of drifting shapes,
  additively blended so overlaps glow.
- **Ornaments** — central mandala, concentric pulse rings, rotating spoke halo,
  orbiting satellites, self-drawing corner fans.

**Color.** Deep violet-black ground, luminous haze ink. The default triad is
magenta + cyan with violet/gold harmonics — but every element is painted from the
theme's accent colors, so a website's brand color (or a user pick) recolors the
whole kaleidoscope.

**Type.** Syne (display) — an arty geometric grotesque — over Inter body.

**Best for.** Launches, art & music, beauty, anything that should feel mesmerizing.
