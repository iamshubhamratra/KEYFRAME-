# Bauhaus Riot

A **print poster that came alive**. Warm cream paper with a dotted halftone
texture, bold primary-color geometry (circles, triangles, squares, a ring), hard
**offset shadows**, sticker **stamps**, and a numbered sheet masthead + frame. The
motion is graphic, not cinematic: **stamps snap flat**, shapes **drop and bounce**,
a three-band **curtain wipe** lifts on the opener, red **X-marks** strike, and a
yellow **highlight** sweeps behind the key line.

## Palette (NO gradients, glows or blur)
- **Paper** `#F4EEE1`, **cream** `#FFFDF6`, **ink** `#17161B`.
- **Primaries** — red `#E4432C`, blue `#2B4BD7`, yellow `#F2C21F` — used flat, in
  blocks and offset shadows.

## Type
- **Display** Archivo Black (heavy, uppercase, tight) with red/blue drop shadows.
- **Chrome** Space Grotesk mono (masthead, sheet counter, kickers, stamps).
- **Body** Inter.

## Motion
- Stamp-ins (`scale 1.9 → 1`), bounce drops, curtain wipes, count-ups, marching
  dashed arrows, X-mark strikes, a highlight sweep, tile flips. 240–900ms, snappy
  eases (power4.in / bounce / back). Deterministic (paused seeked timeline).

## Screenshots
Real screenshots/photos are shown as a **poster-framed panel** — an ink-bordered
cream card with a hard offset shadow, a corner sticker stamp and a color block
behind it, stamped into place.

## Best for
Bold, confident, editorial and design-forward brands — agencies, launches,
statements, culture, sports, music, and anything that wants to shout.
