# Manifesto

Pure editorial typography — an essay that moves. The one pack in this family with no image slots at all.

## Palette

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| ivory (page) | #f6f1e7 |
| ink | #1e1b17 |
| ox (accent) | #7a2e2e |
| gold | #b08d3f |
| paper | #fbf8f1 |

Page and accent are tweakable (ivory → warm #f2e9dc or grey #eeeee6; oxblood → navy #1f3a5f or forest #3e5c3a).

## Type

Playfair Display 500–800 with a true italic sets every headline in sentence case, 92px on montage up to 150px on the statement, line-height 1.06. The hook is **centre-aligned and sits low** (title top 560) with a bare gold kicker above it. Lato 400–700 runs the sub-copy, chips, list rows and stat captions. This is the only pack whose brand mark is a typeset italic serif *M* rather than a drawn icon.

## World

Deliberately almost empty. A 2px rule at 30% ink hangs from y140 to y300 down the centre, ending in a 6px oxblood dot at y330. An italic Playfair asterism (⁂) sits at 40px, 35% ink, near the foot of the page. Behind everything, four 400px outline-only italic 'Aa' glyphs — 2px ink stroke, no fill, 10% opacity — march leftwards at 24px/s and wrap. Ambient clock 1.6, but the world only appears on hook and feature; every other beat is flat colour. `media: false` — the pack accepts no image slots.

## Motion

Cams zoomOut, pushU, pushL, drop, pushR, zoomIn (mul 5, offset 2) with `rot 0.3, drift 4/5, inn 0.3, dz 0.03` — the gentlest camera here. Titles use the **drowse** preset, the softest entrance in the library; items **rise**.

## Beats

- **Typing (typewriter)** — a line assembled character by character with a blinking caret.
- **Morph (fade)** — one word cross-dissolving into the next, no mechanical flourish.
- **Swipe (flip)** — a page turning to the next card.
- **Scroll (board)** — a numbered maxim list scrolling past.
- Statement drops to ink with gold highlights; stats invert to a full oxblood field with gold and ivory numerals at 150px; the CTA is ink with a paper block button and a circular logo lockup.

## Best for

Essays and long-form newsletters, publishing houses and book launches, law firms and strategy consultancies, foundations and institutions, brand manifestos and positioning films, awards and anniversaries — any brief whose whole asset is the sentence.
