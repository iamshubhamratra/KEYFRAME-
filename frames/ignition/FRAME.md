# Ignition — Frame (video design system)

**World.** Mission control, seconds before liftoff. A deep space-blue pad at
night: stars drifting down (the camera rides the vehicle up), hot exhaust
embers rising with a flicker, telemetry ticks scrolling the right margin, a
thin altitude sweep line climbing the frame, and the guidance computer's HUD
locking onto every key word.

**Motion grammar (all bespoke to this pack).**
- **Entrance `countin`** — each headline word drops from high scale under a
  motion blur and brakes hard on the baseline, landing on a deliberate
  countdown cadence: 3… 2… 1.
- **Emphasis `reticle`** — the accent word burns in the ember accent under a
  heat glow, locked inside HUD corner brackets with mid-edge crosshair ticks.
- **Cut `thrust`** — a full-frame column of vertical exhaust streaks rockets
  bottom-to-top across every boundary, its leading edge a hot white plume:
  the frame lifts off between scenes.
- **Canvas `telemetry`** — a two-layer starfield falling as embers rise,
  telemetry tick dashes scrolling the right margin, and an altitude sweep
  line climbing the frame every few seconds (deterministic, seek-safe).
- **Ornaments** — a T-minus countdown ring with a depleting arc, rotating
  sweep hand and stepping mono digits on the hook; a margin telemetry column
  (ALT / VEL / FUEL bars filling to live values); a dotted trajectory arc a
  small vessel climbs on proof beats; a radial engine-start spark burst,
  expanding GO rings and a three-light status row going green on the CTA;
  thin HUD corner brackets locking every frame.

**Color.** Deep space-blue pad ground (#070B14), vapor-white ink. Thruster
ember (#FF5A1F) is the current — the fire of the launch — with signal cyan
(#33E0FF) as the guidance system, caution amber and GO green as telemetry
harmonics. Every ring, streak, spark and bar is painted from the theme's
accents, so a brand color becomes the flame.

**Type.** Chakra Petch (display) — a squared, aerospace-technical face, set
uppercase with open tracking — over Inter, with IBM Plex Mono carrying every
telemetry readout and T-minus digit.

**Best for.** Product launches, version releases, countdown campaigns, reveal
films, startup announcements — anything that ships on a date.
