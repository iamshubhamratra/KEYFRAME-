01 · The thread box

Four threads, one felt

The ground is always night felt — dark, flat, matte. Everything on it is thread: yarn cream reads, marigold emphasizes (once per frame), thread red and teal carry the pattern work. No gradients, no glow, no second surface — if it isn't a stitch, it isn't in the pack.

02 · Block letters

Counted on cloth

Alfa Slab One is the sampler alphabet — always uppercase, cream or marigold only. Inter is the reading thread. Underlines are running stitches, never text-decoration.

03 · The notions drawer

Everything is thread

running-stitch

The atom. A dashed hand-sewn line whose dashes crawl — thread pulled through felt.

cross-stitch-row

A border band of X's, popped in one by one. Sewn under the emphasis word.

zigzag-hem

Rick-rack ribbon edge for hems and patch trims. A line, never a filled ribbon.

patch

A stitched-on fabric card — its dashed seam is its only chrome. Holds stats and captions.

— N× —
figure from script

button

Four holes, a thread cross, slow turn. The largest filled shape the pack allows.

tassel

Knot plus hanging strands, swaying ±6° on a slow sine. Two per frame, at most.

sampler-frame

The traditional border around every scene — a crawling running-stitch rect, corner X clusters, an optional zigzag band. It sews itself on scene entry; the frame is never bare.

04 · Motion grammar

The needle keeps time

Needle punch

Headline characters flip up from flat (rotationX −90°, origin bottom) one by one on a ∼0.07s sewing-machine tick, each with a small settle bounce.

PUNCH IN

The weave

Scenes never cut — a band of bold thread stripes shuttles across like a loom pass, wiping one scene into the next.

Live stitching

The fabric never rests: running-stitch dashes crawl, border X's pop in and hold, buttons turn, tassels sway — all under 30% cream so the motto stays loudest.

Embroidered emphasis

The frame's one marigold word: it punches in, a dashed outline sews one lap around it, and a row of cross-stitch X's stitches in underneath.
