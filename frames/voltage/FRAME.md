# Voltage — Frame (video design system)

**World.** A high-voltage lab at night. Near-black ground charged with electric
light: forking lightning bolts, a Tesla arc snapping between two electrode discs,
charged particles rising through a breathing accent vignette, a glowing
oscilloscope trace running along the base, and a plasma orb throwing sparks.

**Motion grammar (all bespoke to this pack).**
- **Entrance `zap`** — each headline word arrives horizontally stretched and
  skewed under a charge blur, then cracks flat with an aggressive overshoot.
- **Emphasis `volt`** — the accent word glows like a charged conductor and carries
  a jagged high-voltage underline.
- **Cut `strike`** — a jagged lightning bolt forks across the frame under a hard
  white flash on every scene change.
- **Canvas `electric`** — rising charged particles, a breathing accent vignette,
  and a periodic forking bolt (deterministic, seek-safe).
- **Ornaments** — forking hook bolt, crackling Tesla arc, capacitor charge-ring,
  oscilloscope waveform, plasma orb with a spark burst on the CTA.

**Color.** Near-black ground, ion-white ink. The default current is electric cyan
with a violet arc and gold/red spark harmonics — but every bolt, arc and glow is
painted from the theme's accent colors, so a brand color becomes the current.

**Type.** Orbitron (display) — a techno geometric face, set uppercase — over Inter.

**Best for.** Tech & gaming launches, sports, music drops, bold announcements.
