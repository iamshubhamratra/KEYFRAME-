# Rind & Wheel

An affinage cave at a permanent 11°C — gold rind, pale paste, red wax, and a hygrometer that never stops moving.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| cave | #3c3630 | primary ground — hook, feature, stats, app |
| rind | #d9a441 | highlight, chips, CTA ground, half the wheels |
| paste | #f4e8c8 | all type, tile keylines, card lines |
| wax | #8c3a2e | statement ground, cut wedges, hygrometer column |
| ink | #241f1a | montage ground, card fill, cellar arch |

Desk (outer stage) `#2a2420`. App cards lift to `#4a423a`.

## Type

**Bree Serif** — a single-weight chunky slab — sets titles at 94–144px on a 1.08 line, which is why the `stamp` entrance reads as a press rather than a fade. **Sarala** 400/700 runs ledgers, chips, stat captions and drag labels. No forced uppercase; the deck supplies its own caps.

## World

On the right, two 8px paste-white planks at y=300 and y=520 each hold three cheese wheels, built from stacked ellipses: an rx58/ry34 body plus an rx58/ry30 top face, alternating rind gold and paste cream by index. Alternate wheels get a 25%-ink outline ring; every third gets a wedge cut showing wax-red interior. Two humidity droplets fall 500px down the left, shrinking and fading as they go. Bottom-left, a cellar hygrometer: a rounded outline tube, a wax bulb (r22), and a wax column that rises and falls on `sin(0.6t)`, with `11°C` and `94% RH` typeset in Sarala 24px beside it. Over everything, an rx420/ry90 ellipse stroked 60px in 50% ink arcs across the top — the cave mouth. World shows on hook, feature, stats and app.

## Motion

Camera set: `zoomIn, pushL, drop, pushR, zoomOut, hopU` at multiplier 5, offset 1. Magnitudes 0.5° roll, 5px horizontal drift, 0.26 inner ease. Titles **stamp**; items **rise**. Ambient 1.5, tweak default motion `Calm` — busy world, unhurried camera.

## Beats

- **DragDrop / drag** — turning day: wheels are physically lifted and reseated.
- **Toggle / dial** — cave controls: humidity, air change, brine, door.
- **Scroll / board** — the cave ledger, five wheels against their ages (3 wk → 22 mo).
- **Morph / fade** — wheel #88's timeline crossfading CURD → PRESS → BRINE → GLORY.
- **Statement** takes full wax red at 144px; **CTA** flips to rind gold with an ink block button and a circular logo lockup.

## Best for

Cheesemongers, creameries and affineurs; fermentation, charcuterie, natural wine and bakeries; slow-food restaurants, provenance-led producers, and any brand whose selling point is elapsed time.
