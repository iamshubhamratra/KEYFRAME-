04 · The animator's rules

Motion, one pose at a time.

This is the pack no other template shares: nothing glides. Every tween is quantized to visible poses — the browser doing what an animator's thumbs do between camera clicks.

1 · The 8fps law

Every move uses a stepped ease with durations in multiples of 125 ms — a half-second move is exactly four poses. Holds are real holds: between moves, the clay is at rest.

2 · Squash & stretch

Words and props drop in stretched tall, squash wide for one frame on landing, overshoot back and settle — a six-pose landing, staggered word by word.

3 · The smear frame

Scene changes ride a clay blob streaking across the screen, stretching into a long smear at mid-flight — an animator's in-between drawing — covering the cut for two or three poses.
