# Poster Pop

**Quiet doesn't sell.** Solid color blocks, gigantic stamping Caprasimo,
endless marquee tickers and spinning starbursts — the loudest way to say it
in ten seconds flat.

## The rules

1. **Solid blocks only.** Paper, terracotta, sage, ink — full-contrast
   counter-color content, never a gradient.
2. **One boxed word.** Exactly one word per headline in the solid terracotta box.
3. **The ticker never stops.** Top and bottom marquee bands scroll the film's
   own copy at constant speed.
4. **Stamp, shove, spin.** Words stamp; cuts shove panels; starbursts turn.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** Loud ones.

## Purpose

Sales, announcements, events, drops — impossible to miss.
