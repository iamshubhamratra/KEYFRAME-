# Organic Garden

A **place, not a slide deck**. A cream-paper garden runs continuously behind every
scene — drifting petals and leaves, soft blooms of colour, two rolling hills — and
because it never resets at a cut, the film reads as one unbroken moment.

## Palette
- **Paper** `#F5EAD8`, **surface** `#EBDDC5`, **ink** `#201E1D`.
- **Terracotta** `#C67139`, **sage** `#7A8A5E`, **blush** `#FFC6A5`.
- Under a brand skin the entire garden regrows in the brand's hues at the pack's own
  luminances — petals, hills, blooms, chips, buttons and the paper ground itself.

## Type
- **Display** Fraunces — soft, warm, high-x-height.
- **Body** Bricolage Grotesque.

## Motion
A calm continuous camera (drift / swing / push / zoom) runs the whole of each scene, and
the cut is **coverless**: content pushes through the boundary under a short cross-fade
while the garden keeps drifting. Entrances rise and pop on a gentle back-ease.

## Screenshots
Aspect-routed from the asset's real pixels — a browser frame for a desktop capture
(which **scrolls** its page inside the window), a phone for a mobile one, a rounded
shadowed card otherwise. Two or more captures become the tile wall.

## Best for
Warm, human, craft, food, wellbeing and independent brands.
