# Organic Garden

**The garden, filmed.** Warm cream paper, sage hills rolling along the base,
petals drifting down the margins, and Caprasimo headlines that bloom out of
the soil with a seed-ring around the word that matters.

## The rules

1. **Cream + sage + clay.** Ground `#f5ead8`, hills sage `#7a8a5e`, voice clay
   `#b2622d`, soft petal `#dcb28a`. Nothing neon, nothing cold.
2. **One seed-ring word.** Exactly one word per headline wears the dashed
   ring. The rest stays ink.
3. **The hills own the base.** Every scene stands on the double sage hills;
   petals keep falling the whole scene.
4. **Grow, don't slide.** Words bloom up with a springy overshoot; stats grow
   like stems; cuts wash through like a breeze.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** Growth stats and feature chips are lifted
   from the brief — never invented.

## Purpose

Gardens, plants, wellness, eco brands, farm-to-table, skincare, slow living —
your story, grown on screen.
