# Daylight Console

Light-ground editorial landscape layout for software promos: warm paper, contemporary Bricolage Grotesque display, Instrument Sans body, and adjusted vertical offsets sized to fit long script copy in 16:9 format.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F7F4EE` | primary warm off-white ground |
| `linen` | `#EFEAE1` | secondary ground for statement, montage and CTA; card and tile fills |
| `ink` | `#14161A` | all body and display type, tile labels |
| `slate` | `#4A4F57` | hairline rules and subtle background grid layer |
| `indigo` | `#2A4BD8` | lead painted brand accent — kicker pill, CTA glow, tile borders, counters |
| `clay` | `#B0532A` | secondary warm accent — second chip, motes, secondary stat |
