# Paper Cut

A cut-paper collage that keeps rebuilding itself — breathing torn wave layers, spinning offcuts, and a whole palette of full-bleed grounds.

## Type
Shrikhand — a heavy, slabby display serif and the only serif here — sets titles at 94–140px in sentence case, line-height 1.12, with a `pop` entrance. Sen (400–800) handles body, chips, checklist rows and caption typing. Nothing is forced to uppercase.

## World
A layered collage, drawn only on the hook and feature beats — elsewhere the colour field itself is the design. Three torn-paper wave bands fill the lower frame: mustard whose baseline sits at 62% height and breathes ±16 on sin(t·0.5), teal at 72% breathing ±14 in counter-phase, coral at 84% breathing ±12 on sin(t·0.4) — so the layers lift and settle against one another like sheets being handled. At 82% width a two-tone paper sun: a 120-radius coral disc with a 66-radius mustard core. Nine rounded 22px squares in coral/teal/mustard drift right at three speeds, bob 20px on sin(t + i), and rotate continuously at t·40 + i·40 — offcuts falling off the table. Ambient clock 1.6.

## Motion
The camera list is pushR, zoomOut, pushL, hopU, pushD, spin, but stride 3 from offset 2 means only indices 2 and 5 fire — the film alternates **pushL and spin**, nothing else, which is why it feels hand-turned. Roll 1.1 and horizontal drift 9 keep the sheets tilting. Titles `pop`, items `stamp` — pressed down rather than faded in. Grounds cycle cream → coral → cream → teal → ink → mustard, the widest ground rotation in the set.

## Beats
- **DragDrop (assemble)** — the signature: pieces flown into place to build the composition.
- **Typing (caret)** — a hand-set caption or colophon typed with a visible caret.
- **Swipe (swipe)** — palette and cover options swiped through one at a time.
- **Toggle (check)** — studio rules and paste-up readiness ticked off.
- Cards use the `paper` variant (matte #fffaf2 sheet, ink rule, radius 10) and chips are **square** — both unique to this pack; montage tiles tilt hard at ±2–3.5°.

## Best for
Craft and stationery brands, zines and independent publishing, design studios and print shops, kids and education, food and market brands with a hand-made story, and any film that should look like it was made on a table.
