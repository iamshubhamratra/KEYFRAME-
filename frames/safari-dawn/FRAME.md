## Type
Staatliches sets all titles in forced uppercase at line-height 1.0, 98–160px, with a `stamp` entrance that presses each line onto the ground. Assistant (400–800) handles body, chips, feed rows and stat labels. The hook title is parked low at 640px so the sun and the acacias own the upper frame.

## World
A layered savanna, shown on hook, montage and CTA. A 150-radius sun disc with a 230-radius halo whose cy is driven by scene progress, not time — it climbs 140px from beat start to 90% through, then holds. Three white cloud ellipses drift right at 14/20/26 units per second across staggered heights. A burnt-sienna rolling-hill band spans the frame at 55% opacity. Two flat-top acacias — full scale at 16% width, 0.7 scale at 84% — each a thin trunk under a wide quadratic canopy. Three animal silhouettes gallop left-to-right at 46/56/66 units per second, scaled 0.8 to 1.08, bobbing 16px on abs(sin(t·3)) with two stroked legs each. A huge ink ellipse (0.9× frame width, 260 tall) at 92% opacity closes the bottom as a foreground rise. Ambient clock 1.7.

## Motion
Six-move set — zoomOut, pushL, hopU, pushR, spin, pushD — strided by 5 from offset 2, walking the list in reverse so all six moves appear before any repeats. Roll 1.2 and horizontal drift 10: the whole frame tilts, which is what makes the flat silhouettes read as a moving landscape rather than a poster. Titles `stamp`, items `pop`. Feature and stats beats drop the world entirely — burnt sienna and dark ink flats — so the world reads as a place you keep returning to.

## Beats
- **Scroll (feed)** — a sightings log / radio-call feed scrolling under the title.
- **Ring (gauge)** — a golden-hour gauge burning down, or a day-one completion arc.
- **Toggle (switch)** — a 5am kit check, camp rules agreed one switch at a time.
- **Morph (fade)** — one name cross-fading into the next, for species and place names.
- Plus Feature (ink card, cream rule, sun/paper pill chips), Stats (three-column counts on ink) and a cream-tagged Hook kicker.

## Best for
Safari lodges and camps, expedition and adventure travel, wildlife conservation, outdoor optics and gear, nature-documentary trailers, and any brand whose story starts before sunrise.
