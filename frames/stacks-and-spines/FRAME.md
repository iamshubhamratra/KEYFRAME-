# Stacks & Spines

A late-shift reading room: forest-green walls, oak shelves, one banker's lamp, and dust in the beam.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| green | #223828 | primary ground — hook, feature, stats, app |
| paper | #f2ecdc | all type, card faces, tile keylines |
| spine | #a8402e | highlight word, chips, CTA ground |
| oak | #8a6a42 | shelf rails, kicker text, montage highlight |
| lamp | #2e6e4e | lampshade, one book spine in four |

Statement and montage beats drop to `#1a2c20`, a darker cut of the same green. Desk (outer stage) is `#161c16`.

## Type

**EB Garamond** 500–700 sets every title, 92–142px on a 1.08 line — a real book serif, never a display substitute. **Public Sans** 400–700 carries kickers, list rows, stat captions and notice bodies. No `upper` flag anywhere: the deck is authored in caps and the serif keeps it civil rather than shouty.

## World

Two oak rails (10px) span the frame near the floor, 210px apart. Each carries sixteen book spines, 110–166px tall and 30–52px wide, filled from a four-colour rotation — spine red, oak, 85% paper, lamp green — with every seventh volume leaning 7°, and two thin 50%-paper rules top and bottom on each spine, like real cloth banding. Above them a banker's lamp: an oak gooseneck arm, a lamp-green half-dome shade, and a bulb whose opacity breathes 0.7→0.9 on `sin(2t)`, casting an 8%-opacity trapezoid of `#f6e6b0`. Ten dust motes fall through that cone at three different speeds, fading as they drop. World shows on hook, feature, stats and app; statement and montage cut to bare green.

## Motion

Camera set: `zoomOut, pushL, drop, pushU, zoomIn, pushR` at multiplier 5, offset 2. Magnitudes are deliberately small — 0.3° roll, 4px horizontal drift, 0.03 z-drift, 0.3 inner ease. Titles arrive on **drowse** (a settling fall); items and cards **rise**. Ambient clock 1.4 — the slowest world in this group.

## Beats

- **Typing / typewriter** — an acquisitions catalogue card types itself and takes a "filed" stamp.
- **Scroll / stack** — the returns trolley: five titles with a status column, stacking upward.
- **Toggle / check** — closing rounds ticked off one row at a time.
- **Notify / side** — front-desk notices slide in from the frame edge.
- **Statement** takes the frame alone on dark green at 142px; **CTA** lands on spine red with a paper block button and a rounded logo lockup.

## Best for

Libraries, bookshops, publishers, archives and museums; literary festivals, reading apps and journals; any brand whose product is patience, provenance or quiet.
