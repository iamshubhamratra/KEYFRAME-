# Hive & Honey

Hive & Honey — a family apiary in cream and honey, the one pack in this set that stays in sentence case.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| honey | #f7c948 | highlight; floods the statement ground; kicker and CTA pills |
| comb | #7a4f16 | dark amber — montage ground, feature card fill, hex strokes |
| cream | #fff8e8 | primary light ground on hook, feature, stats, app |
| ink | #3a2c14 | all type on light grounds; the CTA ground |
| leaf | #8aa34f | balancing green — chips, stat columns |

## Type
Display: **Fredoka** 500–700 at 94–152px. Body: **Quicksand** 400–700. No beat sets `upper` — headlines run sentence case with a tight 1.08 line height, which is what separates this pack visually from every other in the family.

## World
Runs on hook, feature, stats and app. Right side: six 62r hexagons laid out as a comb cluster, each scaling in on an outBack overshoot keyed to the **scene's own progress** (staggered 0.06 apart) rather than to wall-clock time — so the comb builds once per beat instead of looping. Alternate cells fill honey at 55%, the rest are hollow with a 5px comb stroke. Left of centre, two bees orbit sine paths (26×18 honey ellipses with ink stripes and a white 75% wing highlight), banking up to 24° on cos. Below, a comb bar 150px wide releases a honey droplet that falls 220px on a 0.4Hz loop, shrinking to 60% and fading out. Ambient clock 1.8.

## Motion
Camera set: hopU → pushR → zoomIn → pushL → drop → zoomOut at multiplier **3** — the softest camera here — with offset 1. Magnitudes: 1.3° roll, 9px x-drift, no vertical drift. Titles enter on **bounce**, items on **pop**. Motion default: Lively.

## Beats
- **Toggle — check**: a checklist that ticks itself through a hive inspection.
- **Ring — gauge**: a needle sweep for a capped frame / harvest readiness.
- **DragDrop — drag**: an object is picked up and dropped into a target (jar to crate).
- **Morph — roll**: options roll over one another rather than fading.
- Plus the spine: Hook (honey pill kicker on cream), Statement (full honey ground, cream highlight), Feature (tilted comb card, honey/leaf pills), Montage (comb ground, cream tiles tilted up to 3°), Stats (comb/leaf/ink columns), CTA (dark ink ground, honey pill button, circular logo).

## Best for
Apiaries and farm stands, preserves and pantry goods, garden centres, children's and family brands, wellness and natural skincare — anywhere warmth beats urgency.
