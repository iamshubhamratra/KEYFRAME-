# KICKFLIP CO

Kickflip Co — a DIY park and shop in sun-bleached concrete, ink and one traffic-cone orange.

## Palette
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| crete | #d8d4cc | primary concrete ground on hook, feature, stats, app; type on ink and orange |
| ink | #191a1c | statement and montage ground, card fill, all type on concrete |
| safety | #ff4d00 | the highlight; floods the CTA ground; the board, the spark scrawl |
| sky | #4fa3d1 | cool relief — a chip and a stat column, used sparingly |
| grip | #2e2e30 | charcoal montage tile fill, a shade off ink |

## Type
Display: **Bungee** — a single stacked-sign weight, uppercase at 92–150px, letter-spaced 0.01em. Body: **Rubik** 400–700. It reads as painted park signage rather than editorial type.

## World
Runs on hook, feature, stats and app. One 12px ink path at 35% draws the full bowl profile — a 260r transition on each side joined by a 520px flat — across the whole frame. A skateboard (104×16 orange deck, two 10r ink wheels) travels that profile on a 0.9Hz sine: in the flat it holds an 8° wobble, and inside the outer 15% of each wall it launches up to ~200px and spins through 160°, so the trick lands where the coping is. Top right: an orange grind-spark scrawl — a five-segment 10px zigzag at −8° with a stray 7r dot. Four ink pebbles at 30% scatter across the deck. Ambient clock 2.0 — tied for the busiest here.

## Motion
Camera set: pushL → spin → pushR → zoomIn → hopU → drop, multiplier 7, offset 1. Magnitudes: **6° skew** and **1.5° roll** — the hardest of this group — plus 0.16 inner scale. Titles and items **both** enter on slam, so nothing eases in. Motion default: Bouncy.

## Beats
- **Cursor — keys**: a keyboard-driven cursor walks a combo line through a sequence.
- **Ring — bar**: a linear progress bar for a build/rebuild, not a dial.
- **Notify — pop**: park alerts punch in and out.
- **Morph — roll**: the trick of the week rolls over from one option to the next.
- Plus the spine: Hook (ink tag kicker on concrete), Statement (ink ground, concrete type, orange highlight), Feature (8r framed ink card, square safety/ink/sky chips), Montage (grip tiles at 6r tilted −4° to 3° — the loosest tilts here), Stats (with rule), CTA (full safety-orange ground, ink block button, rounded logo).

## Best for
Skate, BMX and surf shops, DIY park and community builds, youth sport and clubs, hardware and workwear, scrappy local brands that would rather look built than designed.
