# Lock & Legend

Lock & Legend — a mahogany study lit by brass, where the clock is running and the padlock opens on every beat.

## Type

Cinzel (serif) for display — Roman inscriptional capitals, already caps-only — set 90–146px at 1.1 leading with 0.03em tracking, and forced uppercase on every beat. Radio Canada (sans) is the deliberately neutral counterweight for chips, checklists, notes and stat labels.

## World

A study that doubles as the puzzle, behind hook / feature / stats / app. Bottom-right: a keyhole monument in ink on a brass halo, throwing a faint brass light beam 260px upward. Top-left: a wall clock, 74px radius with twelve ticks, an oxblood second hand sweeping at 2.6 rad/s and a brass hour hand at 0.44. Lower-left: a large brass padlock whose shackle rotates and lifts open between 55% and 75% of every scene's progress. Upper-right: two counter-rotating eight-spoke cog wheels in translucent paper.

## Motion

Cameras: zoomIn, pushL, drop, spin, pushR, pushU — stepped by 7 from index 1, so consecutive beats land on very different moves. Magnitudes: rot 0.8 (the spin move reaches ~8°), inn 0.2 — the shortest settle of the calm-to-lively group. Titles enter on `stamp` (1.7× down to 1 with a 6° unwind, outBack), items on `pop`. Card radii are tight (10px), montage tiles tilt ±2°. Ambient clock 1.8.

## Beats

- **Cursor (keys)** — oversized keycaps depress one at a time with their bottom edge compressing, then a payoff word pops: a combination being entered.
- **Toggle (check)** — square checkboxes tick on in sequence with a scaling check glyph.
- **Ring (bar)** — a vertical tube fills from the bottom against a tick scale beside a 130px counting number: time left.
- **Notify (drop)** — hint cards fall from above with a tilt and a brass initial tile.
- Morph falls through to the default odometer roll for the final code.

## Best for

Escape rooms, immersive theatre and mystery experiences, board and puzzle games, whisky and cigar-lounge hospitality, detective podcasts, heritage brands and anything sold as a limited-time challenge.
