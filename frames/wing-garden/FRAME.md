# Wing Garden

A live butterfly conservatory in script type — the calmest pack in the family, and the only one that never shouts in caps.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| leaf | #2e4636 |
| monarch | #f28c28 |
| morpho | #4a7de0 |
| cream | #f4efe2 |
| ink | #24301f |

Leaf carries Hook, Feature, Stats and App; Statement and Montage go to ink. The CTA is the one full-colour wall — solid monarch orange with ink type and an ink pill button under a circular logo.

## Type
Display is Lobster Two, a connected script at 400 and 700, set in SENTENCE CASE at 122px with line-height 1.06 — no beat sets the uppercase flag, which is unique in this family and is what keeps the script legible. Body is Cabin.

## World
Three butterflies cross the frame left to right at 0.08, 0.11 and 0.14 speed, each bobbing on a sin(9·phase) wave and banking up to fourteen degrees. Their wings are two mirrored paths scaled on X between 0.35 and 1.0 at nine flaps per second, with an ink body line and two antennae; the middle butterfly is morpho blue, the outer two monarch orange. Three flowers rise from below the bottom edge on curved cream stems that sway five degrees at 0.9Hz, each carrying a five-petal cream bloom around a coloured centre. Three 60px-wide cream light shafts at 6% opacity rake diagonally from the top right. Ambient 1.7. World runs on Hook, Feature, Stats and App.

## Motion
Cameras hopU, zoomOut, pushR, zoomIn, pushL, drop at multiplier 5, offset 1. Magnitudes: rot 0.8, driftX 9, driftY 8 — wide, slow float with almost no rotation. Titles rise; items pop.

## Beats
- **Toggle (check)** — square checkboxes fill with the highlight and stamp a scaled tick, one row at a time; greenhouse and care checklists.
- **Notify (pop)** — pill-shaped bubbles scale in from 0.3 with a small rotation at staggered left indents, each led by a coloured dot.
- **Scroll (feed)** — a plain vertical feed list of entries.
- **DragDrop (drag)** — a pointer grabs a chip, arcs it into a dashed slot and a tick badge pops.

## Best for
Butterfly houses and conservatories, botanical gardens, zoos and aquariums, garden centres and plant shops, family attractions and day-out tickets, eco and conservation nonprofits, herbal, floral and honey brands.
