## Type
Bebas Neue sets every title in forced uppercase, line-height 0.98, tracking 0.04em — short shouted lines at 104–164px. Instrument Sans carries body, chips and card copy. Statement beats jump to 164px white-on-navy; the CTA sits at 130px on a full flush field.

## World
An SVG cold-plunge site, always drawn on hook / feature / stats / app beats and switched off for statement, montage and CTA. A white snowbank fills the lower third; a navy ellipse is the hole cut in it, with a broken-shard path at its left rim. Two ripple ellipses expand from the hole and fade over a 140-unit cycle. A coral head sits in the water with a white breath-cloud that rises 60px and dissolves on a 0.4Hz phase. Right of frame, a timber sauna: brown box, dark pitched roof, white door panel, an amber window that brightens on sin(t·3), and a smoke curl that scrolls up the chimney on a 40-unit loop. Fourteen snowflakes fall at three speeds with a sideways sine wander. At the far left a stroked thermometer with a coral bulb and a column that rises and falls on sin(t·0.5), labelled "4°" in Bebas. Ambient clock 1.6.

## Motion
Six-move camera set — zoomOut, pushU, drop, pushR, zoomIn, pushL — strided by 5 from offset 2, so it walks the set in reverse and never repeats a move on consecutive beats. Magnitudes are deliberately gentle: roll 0.5, vertical drift 8, inner-push 0.24. Titles use the `rise` preset; items `streak` in laterally. Grounds alternate ice → navy → ice → navy, and the CTA is the only full coral frame.

## Beats
- **Cursor (slider)** — a draggable pace/temperature slider the film moves for you.
- **Toggle (switch)** — protocol switches flipping on, one per line.
- **Notify (drop)** — messages dropping in from the top edge, group-chat style.
- **Morph (roll)** — one label rolling over into the next, for routines and step lists.
- Plus a dedicated **app** look (card #eef6fa on navy rule) for product UI, and Ring / Stats / Montage / Feature for logs, counts and photo grids.

## Best for
Cold-water and sauna clubs, recovery and longevity studios, breathwork and sleep apps, winter swim events, and any wellness brand whose story is a hard morning ritual with a warm ending.
