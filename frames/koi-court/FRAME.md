# Koi Court

Koi Court — a deep teal pond where three fish keep swimming behind the copy and nothing is ever rushed.

## Type

Gelasio (serif) for display — a warm, high-legibility text serif rather than a decorative one — set 90–148px at 1.1 leading. No beat forces uppercase, so mixed-case and sentence-case copy render exactly as authored. Kumbh Sans (sans) handles kickers, pill chips, census rows and stat labels.

## World

A stocked pond, drawn behind hook / feature / stats / app. Three koi — orange, white and gold — swim continuous elliptical orbits at radii 220 / 280 / 340 and different angular speeds, each body a teardrop with a tail that wiggles at 6 rad/s; the orange lead fish carries a dark pond-coloured spot. Three white ripple rings expand and fade at fixed points on a 130-unit cycle. Three jade lily pads (notched-circle silhouettes at different rotations) drift on a slow ±8px sine. A single koi-orange petal spins 400° as it drifts diagonally across the entire frame on a 0.12 clock.

## Motion

Cameras: zoomOut, pushU, drop, pushL, zoomIn, pushR — stepped by 3 from index 2; no spin, no hop. Magnitudes: rot 0.4, driftY 8, driftZ 0.035, inn 0.3 — a slow floating push rather than a cut. Titles enter on `drowse`, items on `rise`. Cards are 22px-round on a darker #0d1a1e panel, chips are pills. Ambient clock 1.5.

## Beats

- **Ring (ring)** — a 560px radial dial with twelve tick marks and a centred counting number: water clarity.
- **Scroll (feed)** — a numbered census panel scrolling itself behind a live scrollbar thumb.
- **Notify (side)** — garden notes slide in from the right, each with a draining accent bar.
- **Morph (fade)** — 150px words cross-fade and scale into one another instead of flipping or rolling.
- Feature frames its card on near-black with koi and jade pill chips; montage tiles tilt ±1.5° with gold labels.

## Best for

Gardens, aquariums and nurseries, tea houses and ryokan, landscaping and pond care, meditation and journaling apps, ceramics and slow craft — brands whose story is measured in seasons rather than launches.
