# Scoop Lab

A pastel ice-cream lab where the sprinkles never stop falling and the type bounces like a scoop landing.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| cream | #fdeff2 |
| pink | #f25d9c |
| mint | #59c9a5 |
| waffle | #d9a05b |
| ink | #33202a |

Grounds are cream for Hook, Feature, Stats and App. Statement takes the whole frame in pink with cream type; Montage goes mint with cream tiles; CTA goes ink with a pink pill button and a circular logo lockup.

## Type
Display is Titan One — a single-weight fat rounded poster face, set uppercase at 126px on the Hook and 150px on Statement, line-height 1.05. Body and all chips, labels and stat captions are Varela Round, so nothing in the frame has a sharp corner.

## World
Twelve sprinkle rectangles (16x6, r3) in pink, mint and waffle fall the full height of the frame at three different speeds, each tumbling continuously through 320 degrees. In the upper right sits a waffle cone: a tan triangle scored with a lattice of 25%-ink strokes, a pink scoop circle above it with a white 50% gloss dot, a scalloped ten-stroke drip edge, one pink drip that detaches and falls 130px shrinking as it goes, and a cherry that bobs on a sin(2.4t) bounce with a green stem. Three concentric pink outline rings at the left breathe on offset sines. Ambient clock runs at 2.0 — the busiest backdrop in its family. World is on for Hook, Feature, Stats and App; Statement, Montage and CTA go clean.

## Motion
Cameras cycle hopU, spin, pushR, zoomIn, pushL, drop at multiplier 3 with offset 2. Magnitudes: rot 1.5, driftX 10, driftY 9 — the loosest hand-held float in the set. Titles use the bounce preset (outBack, 110px lift plus a 0.7-to-1 scale); items use pop. Cuts land hard because the entry overshoots.

## Beats
- **Ring (bar)** — a vertical thermometer tube with a spouted cap and quarter ticks fills to the target; used for freezer and stock checks.
- **Toggle (switch)** — sliding switch rows that flip on in sequence.
- **Cursor (click)** — a pointer walks the frame and clicks a target, for counter and menu walkthroughs.
- **Swipe (swipe)** — cards push sideways with dot pagination.

## Best for
Ice-cream and dessert shops, candy and soda brands, kids and family products, small-batch DTC food, summer promos, anything that wants to read as sweet and hand-made rather than premium.
