# Schematic Blue

Blueprint sheet: hairline grid, pinned small screenshots, mono annotations that type themselves in, and one cyanotype proof beat.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|
| sheet | #eef2f7 | primary plan-sheet ground, light beats |
| mist | #dbe4ef | secondary ground for alternating beats and tiles |
| ink | #101b28 | all body and headline type on light grounds |
| steel | #44607e | grid rules, leader lines, tile borders |
| blue | #14509b | blueprint accent, frames, buttons, inverted stats ground |
| cyan | #4fb8d8 | highlight and counters on the inverted cyanotype beat only |
| rust | #a8451a | single warm mark, reserved for the closing beat |

## Notes

- Hero picture is intentionally small (0.30 x 0.34 of frame) — the composition is the annotation around it, not the image.
- Stats beat is the only inverted ground; it is the template's signature moment.
- Motion budget is deliberately tiny: micro tilt, slow orbit, sub-4px drift.
