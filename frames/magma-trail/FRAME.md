# Magma Trail

A guided hike on hot rock: heavy slab type slams onto a dark basalt ground while a slow volcano works behind it.

## Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| basalt | #1f1a18 |
| lava | #f43f1e |
| ash | #8c8480 |
| bone | #efe6dc |
| ember | #ffa03c |

Basalt is the ground for Hook, Feature, Stats and App; Montage and CTA drop to a darker #161211. Statement is the single hot frame — solid lava with bone type at 148px. Feature and CTA cards glow lava; stat numbers glow.

## Type
Display is Bevan, a heavy slab serif with a single weight, uppercase at 122px on the Hook and 148px on Statement, tracked at 0.01em. Body is Commissioner in four weights, running the ledger rows, chips and field-station notifications.

## World
A broad ash-grey silhouette at 16% opacity fills the lower frame — a five-point crater ridge with a notch at its centre. At the lip a lava tongue path pulses between 0.5 and 0.9 opacity on sin(3t). Four volcanic bombs, alternating lava and ember, launch from the vent on a ballistic path (a sine hump up to 300px, then a ph-squared fall of 500px) and fade as they travel. Three ash plumes rise from the vent over a five-second cycle, growing from r30 to r90 and thinning to nothing. Three ember heat-shimmer squiggles rise on a 120px loop at 30% opacity. Along the bottom a lava dashed polyline (14/12 dash, offset crawling at -50t) reads as a waymarked ridge trail. Ambient 1.8, but almost every element runs under 0.6 — a busy clock over slow motion.

## Motion
Cameras pushU, zoomIn, drop, pushL, spin, pushR at multiplier 7, offset 2. Magnitudes: rot 1, skew 3, driftY 10. Titles use slam (outExpo, scale 2.2 to 1 with a -4deg kick); items pop.

## Beats
- **Ring (gauge)** — a half-circle needle dial with tick marks and a counted number; crater temperature and conditions.
- **Scroll (board)** — numbered rows with dotted leader lines and values that flicker before settling, like a trailhead board or eruption ledger.
- **Notify (drop)** — cards fall in from above with a slight rotation, each with a brand-initial tile; field-station bulletins.
- **Morph (roll)** — the word rolls vertically like an odometer into the next one.

## Best for
Guided hikes and adventure tourism, national parks and geoparks, geology and field science, outdoor gear and boots, expedition documentaries, hot-sauce and fire-adjacent food brands that want gravity rather than gimmick.
