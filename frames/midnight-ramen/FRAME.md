# Midnight Ramen

Midnight Ramen — a counter-only shop from 11pm to 4am: blackened plum, chili, noodle yellow, jade.

## Type
Display: **Kanit** 500–700, condensed, uppercase at 94–152px, letter-spaced 0.02em. Body: **Sarabun** 400–700. Titles enter on **flip** — a menu board turning over.

## World
Runs on hook, feature, stats and app. Three steam plumes rise 800px from the base at the centre, drawn as 16px round-cap `q` curves in steam at 20% falling to zero. Three noodle waves — 12px serpentine paths in noodle yellow at 40/31/22% — scroll left at 90px/s on a 320px repeat, stacked 46px apart along the lower frame. Across the top, three 140×150 hanging panels rock ±5° on 1.1Hz sines, the middle one filled chili at 75% and the outer two a dark plum, each carrying a 26r steam-stroked ring. Right edge: two 260px jade chopstick lines at −32°, swaying ±4°. Ambient clock 1.7.

## Motion
Camera set: pushU → zoomIn → pushL → drop → pushR → zoomOut, multiplier 5, offset 2. Magnitudes: 0.7° roll and 9px **vertical** drift only — the frame rises like the steam does. Titles enter on **flip**, items on **rise**. Motion default: Lively.

## Beats
- **Scroll — board**: tonight's menu scrolls as a board of dishes and prices.
- **Typing — hand**: a handwritten chef's note writes itself out on screen.
- **Ring — ring**: a full circular dial for broth remaining.
- **Swipe — flip**: bowls flip past one at a time to be chosen.
- Plus the spine: Hook (chili tag kicker), Statement (full chili ground, noodle highlight), Feature (16r framed #120d11 card, chili/jade/noodle pills), Montage (12r tiles tilted −2° to 2°), Stats (chili/noodle/jade columns), CTA (full noodle-yellow ground, broth block button, rounded logo).

## Best for
Late-night restaurants and bars, noodle and street-food brands, night markets, delivery after dark, and any counter-service business whose hours are the story.
