# Groove Crate

Groove Crate — a used-and-rare record shop in aubergine black, orange label, rose and teal.

## Type
Display: **Righteous** — one weight, retro geometric, uppercase at 94–152px. Body: **Mulish** 400–800. Titles enter with a **stamp**, which reads as a sleeve being pressed down.

## World
Runs on hook, feature, stats and app. Lower left: a turntable at (220, H−430) — a 110r cream outer rim at 35%, three concentric groove rings (92/72/52r) at 22%, a solid 34r orange label, and a cream tonearm line pivoting from the spindle at 2.4 rad/s. At (430, H−560) a second assembly tilts 24° and sways ±6° on a 0.8Hz sine: a 200px teal arm with a 16r teal pivot and an 18×34 rose headshell at its tip. Top right: five 20×190 sleeve spines fanned at 3° increments in tang/rose/teal/cream at 55%, each bobbing on its own 1.3Hz phase. Left edge: a three-bar orange equaliser jumping on abs(sin) at 2.0, 2.7 and 3.4 Hz, opacity rising with height. Ambient clock 1.9.

## Motion
Camera set opens on **spin** → pushL → zoomIn → pushD → pushR → hopU, multiplier 7, offset 0. Magnitudes: 1.4° roll, 2° skew, 8px x-drift. Titles enter on **stamp**, items on **streak**. Motion default: Lively.
