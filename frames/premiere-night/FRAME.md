# Premiere Night

**Most launches whisper. Yours roars.** A dark warm house, chasing marquee
bulbs, sweeping spotlights, film-strip montages — and Caprasimo titles that
flip up letter by letter with one gold-shimmer word.

## The rules

1. **Dark, but warm.** House `#171514`, panels `#26221f`, paper type
   `#f5ead8`, terracotta + gold light. Never cold blue.
2. **One shimmer word.** Exactly one gold-gradient word per headline.
3. **The marquee never sleeps.** Bulbs chase along the rails in every scene.
4. **Flip, sweep, roll.** Letters flip up in 3D; spotlights sweep; stats roll
   like odometers; cuts wipe like theater doors.
5. **Numbers come from the script.** The box-office report is lifted from the
   brief — never invented.

## Purpose

Launches, premieres, trailers, showcases — ten seconds of spotlight beats ten
pages of specs.
